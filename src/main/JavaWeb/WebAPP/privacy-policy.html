1. Scope and access model

This policy applies to the Finance Workspace browser application, its same-origin application-server requests, the restricted user accounts used to access it, and privacy-related correspondence with the developer. It does not automatically cover a materially modified copy hosted or operated by another person; that operator must publish an accurate notice for their deployment.

Finance Workspace is a personal study project, not a bank, investment adviser, accounting service, payment service, or commercial financial platform. Public account registration is disabled. An account may be created or enabled only by the developer after direct contact and an access decision.

2. Personal data we process

2.1 Account and authentication data

  • Email address or account identifier, display name, and any account details manually supplied or created for access.
  • Password entered at sign-in. The WebAPP sends it to the same-origin application server for authentication and does not store it in localStorage or sessionStorage. Server-side password protection must be implemented by the application server.
  • Session information, including a session identifier held in a server session cookie and the display name/account temporarily held in browser sessionStorage.

2.2 Financial and planning data

  • Asset, investment, cash/deposit, transaction, income, expenditure, currency, fee, return, risk, report, and related financial-management records entered into or returned by the application server.
  • Goals and budgets entered in the planning tools. In the supplied WebAPP these are stored locally in the browser and are not intentionally sent to the server.

2.3 Technical and security data

  • IP address, request time, requested resource, response status, browser/user-agent information, session events, and diagnostic or security logs that may be generated by the web server, application server, database, network, or hosting environment.
  • Language, theme, privacy-mask and auto-lock preferences stored on the device.
  • Cached interface files stored by the service worker/Cache API to make the application shell available more reliably. The supplied WebAPP does not intentionally cache financial API responses.

2.4 Correspondence

If you email the developer, we process your email address, message content, attachments, timestamps, and related correspondence in order to respond and keep an appropriate record.

2.5 Data not requested

Finance Workspace is not intended to collect special-category data (for example health, biometric, religious or political information), criminal-offence data, payment-card data, or government identity documents. Do not enter such information. Financial information is not normally special-category data under the GDPR, but it is treated as highly sensitive.

2.6 Sources

Data comes directly from you, from records associated with your manually authorised account, from your browser/device, and from ordinary server/security logging. We do not buy personal data or obtain it from data brokers.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Browser session data: until sign-out, session expiry, session clearing or closure of the relevant browser session.
  • Device-local preferences, goals and budgets: until you delete them through Finance Workspace or clear the browser’s site data. The controller normally cannot access purely local data unless you transmit or export it.
  • Authorised account and server-side financial records: while access remains active and the records are needed for the learning project. Following verified withdrawal of access or a valid erasure request, deletion or irreversible anonymisation should normally be completed within 30 days, subject to legal exceptions and backup cycles.
  • Security and diagnostic logs: normally up to 90 days, or longer where required to investigate an incident, prevent abuse, comply with law or establish/defend legal claims.
  • Email and privacy-request correspondence: normally up to 12 months after the matter is closed, or longer where needed to demonstrate compliance or handle a dispute.
  • Backups: residual copies may remain in protected backup rotation for up to 90 days and are not restored for ordinary use except for disaster recovery.

Data may be retained longer only where a legal obligation, active dispute, security investigation or legal claim requires it. When retention is no longer justified, data must be securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Security and confidentiality

The project uses data-minimisation and privacy-by-design measures in the supplied WebAPP, including restricted access, no public registration, same-origin requests, no third-party scripts, a restrictive Content Security Policy, no-store API requests, session expiry, an HttpOnly session-cookie configuration, local-only planning data, a privacy-mask option, and an inactivity sign-out option.

The deployed service should also use HTTPS, secure password hashing on the server, access controls, least privilege, patched software, protected backups, logging, and appropriate host/database security. Access to personal data must be limited to the developer and any strictly necessary provider personnel.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a unique strong password, keep your device secure, sign out after use, and do not send passwords or detailed financial records by ordinary email.
